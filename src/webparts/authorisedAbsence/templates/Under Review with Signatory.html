What you need to do

Please complete the following steps to review and make a decision on the request:

  1. Review the information submitted by the student, including the requested absence dates and supporting evidence.
  2. Verify that the reason(s) provided for the absence are accurate and appropriate.
  3. Confirm whether the absence request should be approved or rejected. If rejecting the request, please provide a clear rationale for your decision.
  4. Where applicable, review and validate the absence monitoring conditions.
  5. Select Approve request or Reject request in the application to submit your decision.

What happens next?

If approved: The request will be forwarded to Registry for further processing.

If rejected: The administrative team will be notified so that the decision and relevant rationale can be communicated to the student.
